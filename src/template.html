            Settings

            Best viewed when opened on a PC.

            Look

            LanguageShow the app in English or German.
            ThemeLight or dark page. Scoreboards stay navy either way.
            Player pictureJersey, or player photos with name and number. Rotating picks new poses each time you open a player: front and back side by side, or one at a time on a phone. Double-tap a photo for the next one.
            Lions & Ravens playersInclude or hide the 6 Lions and 3 Ravens players in every list and in the player totals. Team record and runs stay as played. All-time leaders and Hall of Fame always count Eighters only.

            Install as an app

            Android (Chrome)
            1. Open dusiaclaw.github.io/eighters-stats in Chrome.
            2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
            3. The 8-ball icon appears on your home screen; the app opens full screen.
            iPhone / iPad (Safari)
            1. Open the address above in Safari.
            2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
            3. Tap Add.
            Computer (Chrome or Edge)
            1. Open the address above.
            2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or ⋮ › Install Eighters-stats.
