# Changelog

## v3.10 (2026-11-25)
- Rotating player photos are now the default picture.
- On the phone, one photo at a time: front for one player, back for the next.
- Name on the back is easier to read: plain gold letters, no white outline.
- Settings switches show the chosen option clearly: filled, with a check mark.
- A stray hand was removed from one photo.

## v3.9 (2026-11-25)
- Settings › Player picture: Jersey or Rotating.
- Rotating shows two player photos side by side, front and back, with the player's name and number on the back.
- A new front and back pose is picked every time you open a player.

## v3.8 (2026-11-25)
- Players on a wide computer screen: a baseball field next to the name shows where the player played, circles sized by innings.
- Settings › Player picture: choose the jersey or the new player figure (front and back, with name and number).
- Player names never break in the middle; long names shrink to fit.

## v3.7 (2026-11-25)
- Season compare charts: show count stats as season totals, per game or per 7 innings, so seasons compare fairly.
- Players: the player search is back at the top of the page; on a computer the sidebar has one too.

## v3.6 (2026-11-25)
- Home: club totals for every Eighter since 2011 and a club-history list of milestones (1,000th hit, 1,000th stolen base…) with the next ones coming up.
- Home: Hall of Fame and All-time leaders are big buttons on every screen.
- Season compare opens with all seasons and has year-over-year charts: pick any of 22 stats.
- Team: tiles read Games won and Games lost.
- Players on a computer: the player search sits in the left sidebar.

## v3.5 (2026-11-25)
- Marcos Ramirez #12 now has his 2026 games: 3 games EasyScore had booked on other players are moved to him.
- Hall of Fame: 2021 MVP Christian Erhard and rookie of the year Chang Chih Yeh.

## v3.4 (2026-11-25)
- Language setting: the app now speaks English or German (Settings › Language).
- Phone: Hall of Fame and All-time leaders are big tiles on the Home page; the bottom bar has five tabs.

## v3.3 (2026-11-24)
- Every player now has a full name: 21 more names found in old EasyScore line-ups (2011–2023).
- Single-season batting average and on-base % need 15 plate appearances instead of 25.
- Season compare shows only the seasons you pick; one season shows one row.

## v3.2 (2026-11-24)
- New version popup: when an update is ready, choose Update or see What’s new.
- All-time leaders always count Eighters players only (no Lions or Ravens).
- Single-season leaders: a player with the same record in several seasons is listed once with all the years.

## v3.1 (2026-11-24)
- New All-time leaders page: top 5 career and single-season Eighters in every batting and pitching category.
- Home shows the all-time leaders instead of the record tables.
- Hall of Fame: MVP and rookie of the year updated for 2020, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
- Full names for 25 more players from the 2011–2013 line-ups.

## v3.0 (2026-11-24)

- New Hall of Fame with the MVP and rookie of the year for each season.
- New app icon: the 8-ball baseball.
